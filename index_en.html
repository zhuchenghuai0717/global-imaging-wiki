What each protocol entry records

Fields were chosen so an entry can be read straight off the console. Where a vendor does not publish a value, the field is left empty rather than estimated.

Scan geometry
Slice thickness, interval, pitch, reconstructed thickness
Exposure
Tube voltage, tube current, rotation time, automatic exposure control range
Reconstruction
Reconstruction algorithm, kernel, iterative reconstruction level
Contrast
Volume, concentration, flow rate, injection method, scan delay
Dose metrics
CTDIvol and DLP reference values for comparing protocols
Timing
Scan time and breath-hold requirement

Frequently asked

Is this a clinical decision tool?

No. The library is a research and reference resource. It does not provide diagnostic advice, and it does not replace your department protocol or a radiologist's judgement.

Where do the reference values come from?

Publicly available vendor documentation and peer-reviewed literature. Every entry is a reference value, and fields with no published source are left blank on purpose.

Can I reuse the dataset?

The library is free to read. For redistribution, derivative datasets or commercial use, contact us first so we can confirm the terms and the source attributions.

Do you write manuscripts?

No. We do not ghostwrite or submit on your behalf. We assist with methodology, statistics, figures and responses to reviewer comments. The author remains responsible for the content and the submission.

Scope of service

  • Research methodology and data processing support only. No diagnosis, no treatment recommendations.
  • No ghostwriting, no journal submission on your behalf, no guarantee of acceptance.
  • Studies involving human participants require ethics approval and informed consent obtained by the investigator.
  • Send de-identified data only. Do not transmit personally identifiable information.
